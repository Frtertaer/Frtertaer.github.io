    The rule this page counts

    IKEA UK takes an unused item in its packaging, and an item you opened and checked, within 365 days. You need the receipt. Exceptions: food, plants, custom-made goods and items marked as is. The last day is the receipt date plus 365 calendar days. The receipt date is day 0. A purchase on 6 October 2026 has a last day of 6 October 2027.
