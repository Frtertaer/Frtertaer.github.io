    The rule this page counts

    Apple UK: a purchase from the UK Apple Store or apple.com/uk can be returned within 14 days of the delivery date. Some items cannot be returned. This page counts the delivery date plus 14 calendar days. The delivery date is day 0. The till date is not the anchor. Tell Apple by that day; you then have a further 14 days to send the item back.
